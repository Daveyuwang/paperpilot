# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers, scholars, and research leads who need to move from an open question to a defensible, reusable body of work. They work in long-running, evidence-heavy workflows and need to understand what the agent did, why it changed course, which evidence supports each conclusion, and what still requires human judgment.

## Product Purpose

PaperPilot is an AI research workspace and Research Console. It plans and runs bounded investigations, evaluates evidence before synthesis, audits claims after synthesis, preserves versioned artifacts and provenance, and makes recovery and intervention explicit. Success means a user can inspect, resume, branch, or hand off a research run without reconstructing its reasoning from chat history or mistaking activity for verified results.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, calm, candid. The product should feel precise enough for consequential research, composed during long sessions, and honest about uncertainty, incomplete work, budgets, and external execution boundaries.

## Anti-references

Avoid opaque chat-only research, status theatre that labels fast rules as evaluation, decorative AI dashboards, repetitive card grids, dense walls of undifferentiated telemetry, and interfaces that collapse dispatch, acknowledgement, evidence, verification, and real-world effect into a generic success state.

## Design Principles

1. Make the decision loop the primary object: evidence, evaluation, route, repair, and outcome stay visibly connected.
2. Show proof with every status: distinguish generated, persisted, evaluated, accepted, incomplete, and externally unverified states.
3. Preserve research continuity: stable IDs, immutable versions, checkpoints, and resumable actions should remain legible across long runs.
4. Use progressive disclosure for depth: lead with the current decision and open exact evidence, diagnostics, lineage, and budgets on demand.
5. Keep human authority explicit at consequential boundaries while allowing bounded automation inside an approved scope.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA for text, controls, focus visibility, and semantic status communication. Never rely on color alone; support keyboard navigation, screen-reader labels, reduced motion, zoom, and narrow viewports. Long-running state changes must remain understandable without animation or timing-dependent cues.
